# Changelog

All notable changes to Timbre are listed here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0] - 14 November 2026

First public release.

### Library

- Scans the folders you allow with Android MediaStore, reads tags and cover art,
  and keeps everything on the phone.
- Songs, albums, artists, and folder views, with search and six sort orders.
- Fills missing tags from the file itself for formats the system cannot read,
  including WAV.
- Optional hiding of short clips, and per folder include or block.

### Playing

- Background playback with a media notification, lock screen controls, and
  headset buttons.
- Queue with reorder, shuffle, repeat, per song speed, soft fades, and a sleep
  timer that fades out.
- Three player skins: Sleeve, Vinyl, and Spectrum.
- Lyrics from LRC files or your own text.

### Your music

- Playlists, favorites, star ratings, recently played, on repeat, and fresh
  finds.
- Listening stats: minutes, streaks, top artists, albums, genres, and your
  busiest hour.
- A weekly recap story with shareable cards.

### Look and sound

- Auto, light, dark, and black themes, eight accents, and an accent that can
  follow the current cover.
- Frosted bars and motion that stays out of the way.
- Android equalizer with device presets and bass boost, where the phone allows
  it.

### Keeping it yours

- Backup and restore to one file, without ever including your music.
- No account, no ads, no analytics. Cover search is the only network call, and
  only when you ask for a cover.
