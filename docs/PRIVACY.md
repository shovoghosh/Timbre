# Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Timbre is a local music player. It is built to keep your listening private.

## What stays on your phone

- The list of songs Timbre finds, including file paths and tags.
- Playlists, favorites, ratings, and custom lyrics.
- Listening history and the stats built from it.
- Appearance and playback settings.
- Cover images you choose or download.

Timbre stores these in the app's private storage on your device. They are not
sent to us, and we have no server that could receive them.

## When Timbre uses the internet

Timbre only goes online when you open **Change cover** and search for artwork.
The words you typed (usually an artist and album name) are sent to the public
iTunes Search API and the Deezer API. Those services return cover candidates.
Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Nothing else is uploaded. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no crash
reporting service, and no advertising SDK.

## Permissions

- **Audio and media access** is used to list and play the music you choose.
- **Notifications** is used for the playback notification on Android 13 and
  newer. Playback works without it, but the controls may be hidden.
- **Internet** is used only for cover search.
- **Foreground service** keeps playback alive when the screen is off.

## Children

Timbre does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

You can clear the library from Library settings, remove the app to delete all of
its local data, or export a backup and delete that file yourself.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer address listed on the app's
store page.
